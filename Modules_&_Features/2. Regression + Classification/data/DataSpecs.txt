SMARTHEAL THERAPY DATASET - SPECIFICATIONS
==========================================

File        : Dataset.csv
Rows        : 6,000 patients (one row = one patient)
Columns     : 24  (1 ID + 5 body inputs + 5 pain inputs + 13 therapy outputs)
Missing     : none
Type        : SYNTHETIC data, made only for demonstration and academic purposes.
              It is NOT real patient data and NOT prescribed by doctors.


1. WHERE THE DATA IDEAS COME FROM
---------------------------------
- Personal_Health_Data.csv            -> realistic ranges for age, BMI, body fat, skin temperature, diabetes
- Wearable_Sports_Health_Dataset.csv  -> realistic skin/body temperature range
- Pain_Logs.csv                       -> realistic pain level distribution (1-10), pain duration,
                                         pain types (burning/tingling/shooting = nerve pain,
                                         aching/dull = stiffness, cramping = muscle spasm)
- Therapy_Guidelines PDF              -> ALL heat, vibration and TENS parameter ranges

The values were generated randomly but follow the ranges and patterns of these datasets.
The therapy outputs are then decided by simple rules taken from the PDF.


2. INPUT COLUMNS (10 numeric)
-----------------------------
A) User body specs (5)

  Column               Range       Mean   Meaning
  Age                  18 - 85     51.4   years
  BMI                  16 - 45     26.1   body mass index (kg/m2)
  Body_Fat_Percent     6 - 55      32.3   body fat %
  Skin_Temperature_C   32 - 38     34.5   skin temperature in °C (from a wearable)
  Diabetic             0 / 1       -      0 = No, 1 = Yes (882 diabetic patients, 14.7 %)

B) Pain profile (5)

  Column               Range       Mean   Meaning
  Pain_Level           1 - 10      4.8    how bad the pain is
  Pain_Duration_Weeks  0 - 104     23.4   how long the pain has lasted
                                          (12 weeks or more = CHRONIC pain, 2,051 patients)
  Neuropathic_Score    0 - 3       0.5    nerve-pain signs: count of burning, tingling, shooting
                                          (2 or more = nerve pain, 711 patients)
  Stiffness_Level      0 - 10      4.6    joint / muscle stiffness
  Muscle_Spasm_Level   0 - 10      3.5    muscle cramping / spasm

HEAT (3)

4. RULES USED TO DECIDE THE THERAPY (from the PDF)
--------------------------------------------------
HEAT
  High   (46.5 - 48 °C, 10 - 15 min) : pain 7 or more, pain lasting 2+ weeks, not diabetic,
                                       no nerve-pain signs, age under 60, skin below 36.5 °C
                                       (PDF: "above 46 °C, use with extreme care, 15 min maximum")
  Low    (37 - 40 °C, 30 - 40 min)   : diabetic, OR nerve pain (score 2+), OR age 75+,
                                       OR pain 3 or less, OR skin 37.5 °C or warmer,
                                       OR chronic pain with stiffness 6+
                                       (PDF: "long sessions, deep joint stiffness, chronic aches")
  Medium (40 - 46 °C, 20 - 25 min)   : everyone else
                                       (PDF: "general muscle soreness, mild spasms")
  Age 65+ : temperature kept at 43 °C or below (older skin is more sensitive).
  Safety  : no High heat for pain under 2 weeks old (a fresh injury may swell more with heat),
            and only Low heat when the skin is already warm (37.5 °C+ can mean inflammation).
  Inside each range, higher pain and higher body fat give a slightly higher temperature.

VIBRATION
  Frequency Low  (5 - 20 Hz)    : nerve pain, OR diabetic, OR age 70+
                                  (PDF: balance, proprioception, circulation)
  Frequency High (100 - 150 Hz) : chronic pain, pain 6 or less, spasm 4 or less, age under 55
                                  (PDF: tonic vibration reflex, motor rehab)
  Frequency Mid  (20 - 50 Hz)   : everyone else (PDF: pain relief, spasticity reduction)
  Amplitude High (1.0 - 2.0 mm) : BMI 25+ or body fat 28+, AND stiffness or spasm 4+,
                                  AND no nerve pain, AND age under 65 (PDF: deep tissue)
  Amplitude Low  (0.2 - 0.5 mm) : everyone else (PDF: nerve pathways)
  Pattern  : Stochastic Resonance if nerve pain (PDF: "waking up" damaged nerves),
             otherwise Intermittent 5 s on / 2 s off (PDF: prevents habituation)
  Duration : 10 - 30 min (PDF: 0 - 30 min); longer for chronic pain, shorter for age 65+

TENS
  Conventional : pain lasting less than 12 weeks (acute)
                 80 - 150 Hz, 50 - 100 us, Strong But Comfortable, 20 - 30 min
  AL-TENS      : chronic pain, age under 65, pain 7 or less, no nerve pain
                 2 - 10 Hz, 150 - 300 us, Motor Threshold, 30 - 60 min
  Burst        : other chronic pain (more comfortable than AL-TENS)
                 100 Hz bursts (2 - 4 bursts/s), 150 - 250 us, Motor Threshold, 20 - 40 min
  Placement    : Dermatomal if nerve pain, otherwise Local (PDF electrode placement table)
  Higher pain -> higher frequency in the range; higher body fat -> wider pulse (deeper current);
  longer pain duration -> longer session.

A small random variation (about 7 % of each range) is added to the exact values, the way two
therapists might pick slightly different settings. All values always stay inside the PDF ranges.


5. HOW TO USE IT FOR TRAINING
-----------------------------
  Inputs (X) : the 10 input columns (Age ... Muscle_Spasm_Level). Do not use Patient_ID.
  Outputs (y):
    - Number outputs -> regression (Linear Regression, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting)
      Heat_Temperature_C, Heat_Duration_Min, Vibration_Frequency_Hz, Vibration_Amplitude_mm,
      Vibration_Duration_Min, TENS_Frequency_Hz, TENS_Pulse_Width_us, TENS_Duration_Min
      Metrics: MAE, MSE, R2
    - Text outputs -> classification
      Heat_Setting, Vibration_Pattern, TENS_Mode, TENS_Intensity, TENS_Electrode_Placement
      Metric: accuracy
  Suggested split: 80 % train / 20 % test (random_state = 42).


6. LIMITATIONS (mention in the report)
--------------------------------------
- Synthetic data: the therapy outputs come from guideline rules, not from real doctors.
- The models will learn these rules; real clinical use would need testing with medical experts.
- Pacemakers, pregnancy, epilepsy, open wounds and fresh injuries are not covered;
  the app must ask about these and advise the user to consult a doctor.
